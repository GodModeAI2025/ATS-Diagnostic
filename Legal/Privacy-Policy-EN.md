This policy covers the native ATS Diagnostic app for iPhone and iPad. Our website's privacy information also applies when you visit the website. This policy describes the app version and functions outlined below.

## No central collection of diagnostic records

ATS Diagnostic does not require an account. We do not operate an app service that automatically receives your diagnostic history, request contents or certificates. The app does not contain advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not use its diagnostic records for advertising or user profiling.

“Data Not Collected” in the App Store privacy information does not mean that the app makes no network connections. It sends requests you initiate to the selected destinations and processes their responses.

## Servers and network services you select

When you run an inspection, the app connects to the address you enter. Destination servers and participating network services may receive your public IP address, the requested hostname, timestamps and technical connection information. HTTP requests may include the entered URL, path and parameters, headers and request body. Redirects may lead to additional servers.

Credentials, tokens or personal information you include in a request are transmitted to its destination as part of that request. Use them only with destinations you trust. DNS resolution uses the services configured by your device or network, which may receive the queried hostnames. Operators of third-party servers and DNS services are responsible for their own processing.

To diagnose third-party configurations, the app permits plain HTTP and older TLS configurations. Unencrypted traffic may be read or modified by others along its path. Normal HTTPS certificate trust evaluation remains enabled. A successful inspection is not a guarantee of server security or ATS compliance.

Certificate and revocation checks may cause the operating system's trust evaluation to retrieve issuer information, OCSP responses or certificate revocation lists. A revocation check you explicitly initiate may contact services identified by the certificate or its issuer. Their operators may receive your IP address and technical certificate identifiers. Displaying existing Certificate Transparency timestamps and bundled log directories does not automatically transmit your entire diagnostic history to CT logs.

## Processing and storage on your device

The app processes entered addresses and requests, response status and headers, bounded body previews, certificates, fingerprints, timings, connection metadata and original errors. Headers and bodies may contain cookies, tokens or other confidential or personal information.

Completed records remain in local app storage until you delete records or history, or the app's storage limits remove older entries. The history has entry and storage limits, and body previews are bounded. Settings are stored locally. The app uses iOS data-protection mechanisms; these do not replace securing your device.

Device or iCloud backups may contain app data, depending on your Apple settings. The operating system and Apple manage these backups; they are not an ATS Diagnostic backend. Deleting local records does not automatically remove copies from existing backups or exports.

## Local network

Local-network access is used to inspect devices and servers you select. You can grant or revoke the system permission in iOS settings. Local inspections may be limited without permission. Internet inspections generally do not require this local-network permission.

Since version 2.0.1, the app no longer includes a local HTTP proxy. It does not capture other apps’ traffic, install a root CA or provide a VPN service. Any proxy-rule files left from an earlier version are not loaded or applied and are not automatically deleted by the update.

## Exports, sharing and external links

Reports, HAR files, certificates and other exports are created or shared through your actions. Review their contents before sharing. Recipients, selected apps and cloud services may subsequently process and retain the exported data under their own policies. We cannot retrieve or delete copies you have already shared.

Opening an external link sends the information required for that visit to the website operator. Its privacy information also applies.

## Notifications and Apple services

At your request, the app can schedule local certificate-expiry reminders and asks for notification permission. Reminder information may appear on your lock screen depending on your settings. You can revoke permission in iOS settings and manage scheduled reminders in the app.

Apple processes downloads, purchases, device backups and any system diagnostics you elect to share under Apple's privacy terms. We do not integrate an automatic crash-upload service. Feedback you voluntarily send through TestFlight or directly to us may include screenshots, technical information and contact details.
