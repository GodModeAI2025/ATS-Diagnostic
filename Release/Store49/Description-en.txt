Inspect HTTP connections, TLS negotiation and certificates directly on your iPhone or iPad.

ATS Diagnostic helps you understand user-selected local and Internet endpoints:
• Certificate chain, issuer, validity and fingerprints
• Apple system trust results and original connection errors
• Negotiated TLS version and cipher, plus individual TLS-version probes
• Certificate Transparency timestamps and dated log directories
• Response headers, HSTS explanations and bounded response previews
• Request building, cURL import, history and HAR export
• Foreground loopback HTTP proxy and opaque CONNECT forwarding
• System IPv4/IPv6 address lookup and SHA-256 download checks

Results describe the observed connection. Root-store directory membership is separate from platform trust validation. SCT signatures and log inclusion are not independently verified. A successful request is not a comprehensive ATS-compliance certification.

History stays on your device. Export only the reports you choose to share.
